# Lesson 2: Mastering Customer Service Interactions with AI

**Program:** Per Scholas — AI Skills for IT Support  
**Project:** Aperta IT Help Desk — Printer Support Case  
**Status:** Guided practice and AI response review

## Project Overview

In this lesson, I practiced using ChatGPT to draft and evaluate customer service responses for IT support situations. The focus was on communicating professionally with frustrated customers while ensuring that AI-generated information was accurate and supported by evidence.

## Scenario

Lena, a staff member at the Newark site, reported that office printer NWK-MFP-02 was producing blank pages, including copies made directly from the scanner glass.

Previous support interactions had not resolved the issue, and the customer was frustrated. An onsite technician visit was scheduled, with a backup printing plan available if the printer remained unusable.

## Tools and Skills Used

- ChatGPT
- AI-assisted customer communication
- IT support ticket documentation
- Evidence-based claim verification
- Customer service and empathy
- Technical troubleshooting analysis
- Quality assurance

## Work Performed

1. Reviewed the customer's complaint and support history.
2. Identified the audience, issue, required tone, and facts that needed verification.
3. Used ChatGPT to draft a customer-facing support response.
4. Reviewed the AI-generated claims against the available reference information.
5. Identified wording that could create unsupported expectations about the technician's arrival.
6. Evaluated the response for accuracy, empathy, and clarity.
7. Considered the customer's request not to perform additional printer troubleshooting before the onsite visit.

## Issues Identified

One AI-generated claim described the technician as committed to arriving at 13:30. However, the calendar supported a scheduled Newark visit from 13:30 to 15:30, not a guaranteed arrival at exactly 13:30.

This distinction matters because inaccurate promises can create unrealistic customer expectations and further damage trust.

## Recommended Corrections

- Describe the onsite visit as scheduled rather than guaranteeing an exact arrival time.
- Avoid suggesting troubleshooting steps that are not supported by the available documentation.
- Respect the customer's request not to touch the printer before the onsite visit.
- Explain the backup printing arrangement without promising that the original printer will be repaired.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Customer service communication
- Active listening and empathy
- AI-generated content review
- Technical documentation
- Risk identification
- Evidence-based decision-making
- Managing customer expectations

## Lessons Learned

AI can help draft clear and professional customer responses, but its claims must be checked against the original support records.

I learned that effective IT support communication requires both empathy and accuracy. A reassuring response should explain confirmed next steps without inventing solutions or making promises that the support team cannot guarantee.
